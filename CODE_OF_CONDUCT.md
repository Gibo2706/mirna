# Code of Conduct

Participation in Mirna is expected to remain respectful, constructive, and
focused on the work.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss technical decisions and reviews in good faith.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept correction.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Use synthetic reproductions whenever possible.
- Treat personal financial records as especially sensitive.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, intimidation, and
deliberate disruption are not accepted. Neither are doxxing, intentional
disclosure of credentials or private data, or pressure to publish personal
financial evidence.

## Handling problems

The project maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or
restrict participation when necessary. Platform-level abuse may also be
reported through GitHub's reporting tools.

Do not publish security vulnerability details as a conduct report. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and never attach private financial records.
